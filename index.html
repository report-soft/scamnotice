The Review Process


In our review of a platform, we look at the claims it makes, company ownership, registration details, and contact information.


We then look at the platform's terms and conditions, the withdraw conditions and whether users can withdraw at all, whether there are initial deposits required, and whether there are available reports or complaints about the platform.


Finally we check if the platform displays characteristics commonly associated with fraudulent schemes.


Editorial Standards


We understand that a wrong review can have serious consequences for the platforms we review. This is why we ensure that all reviews are based on thorough research and investigation.


Our reviews are written after we have tried the platform ourselves. We verify the information provided by the platform, as well as check for any complaints or reports about the platform from other users. These complaints and reports are usually available publicly.
