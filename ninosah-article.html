NINOSAH

Ninosah Review: Is It Legit or a Scam?


Ninosah is an online earning platform on which users earn money by completing tasks. We signed up and used Ninosah for some days. This review will help you determine if Ninosah is a legitimate earning platform or a scam.


What Is Ninosah?



Ninosah is an online earning platform that rewards users for completing micro-tasks. Payments are done via Mobile Money.


Users can earn from a variety of tasks including chatting with foreigners, daily games, free spin, watching tiktok & Youtube videos, and listening to spotify music.


Ninosah also has a loans and investments feature where users can take loans or invest and earn interest. This then makes Ninosah a platform for earning from tasks as well investment.


How Does Ninosah Work?


User Sign Up



New users join Ninosah by signing up for an account via ninosah.com. Required details include a phone number registered on either MTN or Airtel Mobile Money and email adddress.


Account Activation



On successful account creation, new users are required to pay an activation fee of ugx 19,000. This payment is done through Airtel Money or MTN Mobile Money.


This money is not an investment and therefore does not reflect on the users' funds account. It only functions to activate the user's account.


Is Ninosah Legit?


Domain Analysis


Ninosah domain is very recent. According to Scam Detector, the domain was created on 27th July 2026, barely three months old.


Contact Details


Ninosah doesn't display any contacts on their website. The website has no contact form, no telephone, no physical address. There are no social media buttons. There is an isolated support button in the footer that opens an email to kapitech.business@gmail.com. No reply.


This means there is completely no way to get support from Ninosah. Your money is simply at the mercy of their website staying online.


Investment Options



Ninosah mentions the investment option as an afterthought. They don't to explain how this investment works, the ROI, or terms and conditions.


The rest of the earning methods are Paid-to-Click tasks that even when genuine pay a very small amount of money. Recovering the upfront investment alone will take a long time.


Withdrawal Options


Ninosah allows users to withdraw earnings, but they are required to have met unspecified minimum earnings. Withdraws also need to be approved by an admin.


Because Paid-to-Click tasks pay very little, users will find it difficult to hit the minimum withrawable amount.


User Reviews


Ninosah has no review, whether on the website itself or from other places online. The only reviews are those made by their influencers on tiktok.


Registration & Licensing


Since Ninosah has an investment option, it must be licensed and regulated by Uganda's financial authorities. It's however not listed among licensed Fund Managers or Recognized Investment Schemes by the Capital Markets Authority of Uganda and Uganda Retirement Benefits Regulatory Authority.


How To Use Ninosah Safely


There is no safe way to use Ninosah, as it has all signs of a scam website. Please avoid it at all costs.
