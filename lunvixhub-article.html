LUNVIXHUB

A user's Hands-on Experience with LunvixHub.


This is an honest review of LunvixHub. We signed up and used LunvixHub for some days, so you don't have to. Read through before you join this investment/earning platform.


What Is LunvixHub?


LunvixHub is an online earning platform where users earn by performing tasks. It promises earnings between UGX 30,000 to UGX 150,000 daily.


The platform claims to be active in 30 African countries. In Uganda alone, LunvixHub claims to have so far paid UGX 1.2 Billion.


LunvixHub users can earn from a number of tasks including chatting with foreigners, watching videos, playing online games, listening to music, answering trivia, clicking paid ads, viewing WhatsApp statuses, downloading & testing apps, daily spins, paid surveys, and instant loans.


How Does LunvixHub Work?


Account Registration



New LunvixHub users join by signing for an account. Required details include an email address, phone number, and choosing a username.


Initial Deposit


LunvixHub users are required to pay UGX 20,000 as account activation fee. This amount is deposited via Airtel Money and MTN Mobile Money. LunvixHub however does not show this initial deposit on their website.


Is LunvixHub Legit?


Domain Analysis


Scam Adviser gives LunvixHub a low rating because it has low traffic, it's hosted on a suspicious server (one known for hosting malicious website), and it has just been established recently (2 months ago).


Although the domain is new and has low traffic, the LunvixHub website claims they have more than 50,000 active users.


Initial Deposit


Although LunvixHub explicitly says no investment is required to use their platform, the user is required to pay an activation fee of ugx 20,000 after signing up.


The initial deposit is received through MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. Suprisingly, the transaction details for LunvixHub are exactly similar to those of KivaniSoft. This is worrying because it makes it look like a coordinated scheme.


Contact Details



LunvixHub does not display any contact details on their website. There is no telephone contact, no email address, no physical address (location).


The website has a link to WhatsApp but the link leads to a WhatsApp Channel. The Channel only allows admins to post, and therefore there is no way to get other users' experience or raise concerns.


For an investment platform, contact details should be a bare minimum!


Investment Options



LunvixHub claims its users can earn from a number of tasks including chatting with foreigners, watching videos, playing online games, listening to music, answering trivia, clicking paid ads, viewing WhatsApp statuses, downloading & testing apps, daily spins, paid surveys, and instant loans



They claim to pay UGX 3000 per video, UGX 5000 per survey, and UGX 2500 per game level reached. These figures are unrealistic. Youtube pays a maximum of $0.04 per view. Majority of surveys in Africa barely reach $1 per survey.


Licensing and Regulation


We searched regulatory data about LunvixHub licensing but found nothing. LunvixHub is not registered or licensed by the Capital Markets Authority of Uganda (CMA) and the Uganda Retirement Benefits Regulatory Authority (URBRA)


User Reviews



LunvixHub reviews on the website are fake. Reviewers' names are generic first names including Sarah, Micheal, Aisha, Daniel, Ruth, Joyce. All revies are 5 Star.


A Kenyan businessman, David. T, leaves a 5 Star review showing he has already earned more than UGX 30,000. Uganda Shiilings, not Kenyan Shillings.


LunvixHub displays fake real-time user activity by displaying withdraws, deposits, and account activations. However, the same withraws, deposits, and account creations are displayed everyday.


How To Use LunvixHub Safely


Do not use LunvixHub hoping to invest and earn. You will not be able to withdraw the earnings you accrue. The website will not process withdraws until they close. Beware of Tiktok influencers.
